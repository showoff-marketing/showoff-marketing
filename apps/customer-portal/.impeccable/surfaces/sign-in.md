# Customer Account Entry

**Mode:** Operate<br>
**Thesis:** A centered form keeps account access direct while the progress row shows where workspace setup begins.<br>
**Story:** Sign in or create an account, name a workspace, then reach the customer shell. Team invitations are outside this setup flow.<br>
**First viewport:** Wordmark; Account / Workspace / Team progress; a compact sign-in panel with Google, Apple, email, password, Continue, and Create account.
**Form:** The user-selected customer comp at `../mocks/selected.png` is the visual reference.

## Quality Bar

- Account entry is clear within seconds, and the current progress step is distinct.
- Provider actions and labeled fields keep legible boundaries and focus treatment.
- Desktop stays centered; mobile keeps every control visible without horizontal overflow.
- Do not imply that a team-invitation screen exists in Week 1.
