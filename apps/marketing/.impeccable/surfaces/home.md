# Marketing Prelaunch Home

**Mode:** Persuade<br>
**Thesis:** One headline and a connected process row explain Showoff before the waitlist action.<br>
**Story:** A first-time visitor understands the seven-stage marketing workflow and can find the waitlist state.<br>
**First viewport:** Showoff / Prelaunch masthead, “Marketing, all in one place.” headline, concise workflow description, seven blue outline stages, and a single Join the Waitlist action.
**Form:** The user-provided marketing comp at `../mocks/user-selected.png` is the visual reference.

## Quality Bar

- Within seconds, visitors understand what Showoff does, why the workflow is useful, and what to do next.
- Desktop keeps all seven stages in one horizontal sequence; mobile preserves their order in a readable two-column grid.
- The waitlist submit action stays disabled and clearly labeled while its endpoint is not configured.
- Do not add unsupported customer proof or performance claims.
